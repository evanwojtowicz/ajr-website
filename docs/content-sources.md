## Source distinctions retained

- Machine dimensions are labeled “Machine size” by the source. Axis order and usable-part limits are not defined. CMM values are not relabeled as measuring ranges. Only the Mori-Seiki MR2000G and MG915 source entries explicitly specify diameter.
- Two VU65A entries have 59 × 27 × 24 in. sizes and are grouped as two machines; the third has 50 × 25.5 × 24 in. and remains separate.
- The jig-bore text lists model 6CN; its source photograph caption says 6N. The source discrepancy remains marked for confirmation.
- Pressure-test text states “10,000 LBS.” It does not establish PSI.
- Facts clearly gives 22,000 square feet and resolves the equipment page’s malformed “22,0000” entry.
- Supplier requirements mentioning NADCAP are not evidence of an AJR NADCAP certification. CMMC is described as ongoing work, not a completed certification.
- News dates are historical. The Makino F9 caption “Fully operational 5/24” does not explicitly specify a year and is not converted into May 2024.
- Vendor terms were compared clause-by-clause to the live source. Apparent awkward wording in clauses 20 and 22 is retained, and supplier record retention remains ten years / forty years for Flight Safety Parts, as stated.
- The 12 History & Parts photographs were supplied by the user in `part photos.zip` and imported byte-for-byte. Their uploaded filenames, original captions, dimensions, and website paths are recorded in [part photo inventory](part-photos.md).
- The 11 equipment photographs were supplied in `machine photos.zip` and imported byte-for-byte in mixed JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats. Their mappings are recorded in [equipment photo inventory](equipment-photos.md). The Crysta Apex image is not assigned to a specific model in the three-CMM inventory. The source image host is separate from the AJR HTML; supplied ZIP files provide the website assets.
- Nine News photographs and the original Facts-page DNV mark were supplied in `news photos.zip` and imported byte-for-byte. The four F9 and two DMU 50 slides retain their original grouping. The award photograph is marked 2024; the banner is marked 2021. The separate 2021 glass trophy was subsequently supplied in `missing award.zip`, imported unchanged, and placed before the banner in its article. Its inscription confirms 2021 and achievement in Collaboration. All News photographs shown in the supplied source references are now included. See [News photo inventory](news-photos.md). The DNV mark is placed on Quality without recreating it or inventing current certificate details.

Company source statements are not independent verification of current staffing, certification validity, or approval scope. The redesign avoids inventing dates, customers, specs, contact destinations, or credentials.
