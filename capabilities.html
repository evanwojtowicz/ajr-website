Our capabilities

01

5-Axis CNC Machining

Machining complex geometries for demanding components, supported by Makino, Mitsui-Seiki, DMG Mori, and Bridgeport / Hardinge equipment.

02

Jig Boring & Precision Milling

Machining critical bores, housings, structural fittings, and precision assemblies using jig-boring and vertical-milling equipment.

03

Turning

Turning for rotational components, with equipment sizes listed alongside AJR’s machining centers and jig borers.

04

Pressure Testing

Pressure testing for aerospace housings, including high- and low-pressure requirements. Confirm application-specific test conditions and rig capacity directly with AJR.

05

High-Pressure Purging

D.E. Smith high-pressure purge equipment uses 10-micron and 2-micron filtration for internal cores of castings.

06

Inspection & CMM

Dimensional verification is supported by Mitutoyo Strato 910, CRT-Apex 7106, and CRT-Crysta 9106 coordinate measuring machines.
