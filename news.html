Company news.

Recognition, equipment investment, and milestones at AJR Industries.

2024 performance · Recognition

RTX Premier Award for Collaboration & Customer Service.

RTX recognized AJR Industries with a Premier Award for performance in 2024 and excellence in Collaboration & Customer Service.

The award is part of the RTX Performance+ Program, recognizing supplier performance and value. AJR shared the recognition alongside its 60th anniversary in 2025.

2021 performance · Recognition

Raytheon Technologies Premier Award for Collaboration.

Raytheon Technologies recognized AJR Industries with a Premier Award for performance in 2021 and overall excellence in Collaboration.

The recognition was part of the Raytheon Technologies Performance+ Program.

2021 · Capacity expansion

Makino F9 joins the shop floor.

AJR announced the addition of a Makino F9, reporting an additional 6,000 machining hours per year of capacity.

The announcement listed an installation date of May 7, 2021. The accompanying photographs show delivery, setup, and the installed machine.

2019 · Capacity expansion

DMG Mori DMU 50 expands machining capacity.

AJR added a DMG Mori DMU 50, reporting an additional 6,000 hours per year of large-capacity, 5-axis machining.

The machine was installed on July 22, 2019 and operational on July 30, 2019.

2017 · Recognition

Supplier Gold recognition.

AJR reported Supplier Gold recertification with United Technologies Corporation (Raytheon) for 2017, marking its fourth Supplier Gold recognition.

The announcement highlighted quality, delivery, lean manufacturing processes, and customer feedback over a 12-month period.
