About AJR

Precision manufacturing since 1965.

AJR Industries manufactures high-precision components for aerospace, defense, scientific, and advanced manufacturing applications.

AJR Industries, Inc.

Experience. Equipment. Capability.

Founded in 1965, AJR operates from a 22,000-square-foot facility in Elk Grove, Illinois.

Capabilities include complex castings, forgings, production machining, precision milling, turning, specialized processing, and CMM inspection.

AJR serves a national customer base across critical industries.
