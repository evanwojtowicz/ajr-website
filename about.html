About AJR

Precision manufacturing since 1965.

A family-owned manufacturer of complex components for aerospace, defense, scientific, and advanced manufacturing applications.

Our company

Complex machining. Long-term relationships.

Founded in 1965, AJR Industries operates from a 22,000-square-foot, fully temperature-controlled facility in Elk Grove, Illinois.

Machining of complex castings is a core business. AJR also manufactures parts from forgings, bar, and plate stock for a national customer base.

Long-term manufacturing relationships bring together machining capability, production planning, and customer-specific program support.

Investing in machining skills

AJR has an apprentice training program for machinists. Its published production schedule lists two shifts, 22 hours per day, six days per week.

People, process knowledge, and manufacturing experience support work from development through production and aftermarket requirements.

Program support

Supporting the complete part.

AJR’s program support includes purchasing castings and hardware, coordinating outside processes, and shipping completed parts.

Support spans shop offload, pre-production and development, production, and aftermarket or spare-part requirements.

Epicor M.R.P. supports production updates and customer scheduling. Manufacturing partner arrangements include long-term agreements and procurement coordination.
